    The 5 tools

    Five ways to build more connection – for big feelings, clear limits and your own capacity.

    1. 1

      Special Time

      Give your child 5–20 minutes in which they lead. This builds safety, trust and deep connection.

    2. 2

      Setting Limits

      Stop off-track behaviour with love, closeness and respect — without punishment or shame.

    3. 3

      Staylistening

      Stay present through big feelings, so your child can move through them and return to balance.

    4. 4

      Playlistening

      Use play and laughter to release tension, fear or anger and bring more ease into family life.

    5. 5

      Listening Partnership

      Exchange listening time with another parent to release emotions and create capacity for your child.

    About Julia

    I am Julia, a mother of three and a certified Hand in Hand Parenting Instructor. I know real family life — overwhelming mornings, meltdowns, sibling arguments and the moments when you wonder whose need should come first.

    I live what I teach. The tools have changed our family life — not because everything is easy now, but because we find our way back to one another sooner.

    Read more about my journey

    Being a mother was always a heartfelt wish. Yet at the beginning it was far harder than I expected. During my first pregnancy I worried whether I would be good enough and able to offer the closeness and safety I wanted for my child.

    While looking for guidance, I immersed myself in attachment-oriented parenting and personal development. When my son was born, everything became more intense. That was when I found Hand in Hand Parenting — and it fundamentally changed the way I parent.

    I have integrated these tools into our family life for years. There are still difficult moments and times when I fall back into old patterns. Listening Partnerships, coaching and meditation continue to be part of my own lifelong journey.

    In my courses I bring together the clear, loving tools of Hand in Hand with the inner capacity that grows as we understand ourselves better. Parents do not have to do this alone. This work carries us when we do it together.

    Ask your question
    What if I miss a session?

    Missing a session does not have to stop you from joining. I will send you a recording of the theory part and all the written materials so you can catch up.

    Only the theory part is recorded. Discussion, exercises and Listening take place together during the session. If you miss it, you miss that practice and the chance to bring your own questions and situations to the group that day.

    What happens in a session?

    We begin with your experiences and questions from trying the tools at home. I then usually introduce a new tool, which we explore through practical exercises.

    Each session also includes Listening time: a space to be heard yourself and to practise listening to others. Depending on the questions, we take two to two and a half hours.

    What do I do between sessions?

    You try the new tool with your children and bring your questions to the next meeting. You also arrange a Listening Partnership with someone else from the course once a week.

    Written materials help you explore the theory in more depth. Reading is optional, but setting aside time for it helps you get the most from the course. You will meet like-minded parents and build a Listening Partnership that you can choose to continue afterwards.

    What personal support is included?

    A 60-minute individual coaching session with me is included. We arrange it at a time that works for you, during or after the course.

    Your course also has its own WhatsApp group. You can ask questions about everyday situations, and I respond personally, often by voice message. You also learn from the other participants’ questions and experiences.

    What ages is Hand in Hand suitable for?

    The course is especially helpful when your child is discovering their independence and big feelings are shaping family life. Families with older siblings can benefit too – it is never too late to strengthen your connection.

    If you are caring only for a baby or older teenagers, please contact me before registering. Their needs and everyday challenges are different, so we can explore which support would fit your family.

    Can my children be with me during the course?

    Please plan for uninterrupted time without your children. Exercises and Listening especially need your attention and a private space where you can speak openly.

    Breastfeeding a baby during the theory part is fine. Please arrange care for your baby as well during exercises and Listening time.

    Who can join the Starter Class?

    I welcome parents, grandparents and other important caregivers. Friends are very welcome to join together. If you are raising a child together, you attend separate courses. This gives each of you your own space to speak openly, especially during Listening.

    Do I need prior experience?

    No prior knowledge is needed. And if you have already read Listen or know the five tools, the course gives you space to deepen your practice.

    Understanding the theory is a starting point. I support you in putting it into practice through exercises, your questions and real situations with your child. That is how familiar ideas become your own experience.

    How is Hand in Hand different from other approaches?

    We look beyond behaviour to your child’s feelings and needs. Anger and fear are common reasons to seek support. The tools also help you stay connected through sadness and other big feelings.

    Your own needs matter too: being heard and supported gives you more capacity to accompany your child with warmth.
